Loan options

Every buyer's situation is different. Here's where most of my clients start, and I'll help you figure out which fits you best.

First-time buyers

Down payment options, assistance programs, and a step-by-step plan so nothing catches you off guard.

Conventional

Flexible terms for buyers with solid credit, with options for a range of down payments.

FHA

Government-backed loans with lower down payment and more flexible credit guidelines.

VA

For eligible veterans and service members, often with no down payment required.

Refinance

Lower your payment, shorten your term, or tap your home's equity when the numbers make sense.

Jumbo

Financing for higher-priced homes above standard loan limits.
