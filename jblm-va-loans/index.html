Why so many JBLM families buy with a VA loan

The VA home loan is one of the best benefits you've earned. Here's what it can do for you, and I'll help you see whether it beats your other options.

Often no down payment

Qualified buyers can often purchase with $0 down, so you can keep your savings for the move, the deposit on movers, or that first trip to Costco.

No monthly mortgage insurance

Unlike most low-down-payment loans, VA loans don't carry monthly PMI, which can make a real difference in your payment.

No VA loan limit with full entitlement

If you have full entitlement, the VA doesn't cap how much you can borrow. Your budget comes down to what you qualify for.

Use it more than once

Your benefit can be used again on future PCS moves. Depending on your entitlement, you may even keep this home as a rental when you leave.

JBLM home buying questions

Can I buy a home near JBLM before I arrive?

Often, yes. With your PCS orders in hand, we can start the pre-approval process from your current duty station, and much of the paperwork can be signed electronically. VA loans do require that you plan to live in the home, so we'll talk through your report date and move-in timing up front.

How much house can I afford with my BAH?

Your BAH is one piece of your qualifying income, along with base pay and any other steady income. Every family is different, so I'll run your actual numbers, including taxes, insurance and any HOA dues, and show you a payment you're comfortable with, not just the maximum you could borrow.

Do I have to pay the VA funding fee?

Most VA borrowers pay a one-time funding fee, which can usually be rolled into the loan. Veterans who receive VA disability compensation are generally exempt. The fee is lower if you put 5% or more down. I'll confirm your exact fee from your Certificate of Eligibility.

Can I keep my JBLM home as a rental when I PCS again?

Many military families do. A VA loan requires that you live in the home when you buy it, but it generally doesn't require you to sell when you get new orders. Whether you can use your VA benefit again on your next home depends on your remaining entitlement, and I can walk you through that math.

My spouse will be house hunting without me. Is that OK?

Yes, this happens all the time. We'll plan ahead for signatures, and in some cases a power of attorney specific to the purchase may be needed. Let me know your situation early so we can set it up correctly.

Do you only do VA loans?

Not at all. I help first-time buyers, move-up buyers and people refinancing with VA, FHA, USDA, conventional and jumbo loans. As a veteran and military spouse, VA loans are just close to my heart.
